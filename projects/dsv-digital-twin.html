DSV Singapore

Warehouse Digital Twin Command Centre

A command centre web app that links inbound and outbound warehouse data, so workers can trace each shipment and carton in one place.

Role
Data Analytics Developer
Timeline
Sep 2026 – Dec 2026
Type
Industrial project
Location
Singapore

Overview

At DSV's Singapore warehouse, inbound and outbound records were kept in separate Excel files, so tracing a shipment meant searching both by hand.

I analysed a week of warehouse data, found where the two files could and could not be linked, and engineered the main prototype of a digital twin command centre that brings everything into one view, with alerts for orders likely to be late.

What I did

  • Analysed one week of DSV warehouse data (65K outbound and 58K inbound records) and found only Milkrun cartons shared an ID across both files, so workers traced other shipments by searching separate Excel sheets.
  • Engineered the main working prototype of a digital twin command centre web app that traces 5.5K Milkrun cartons from receipt to handover and shows all other shipments and cartons in one place.
  • Added rule-based alerts that flag orders at risk of delay, after finding 23% of 19K orders were handed over after their planned date, most by one day.

Result

5.5Kcartons traced from receipt to handover
