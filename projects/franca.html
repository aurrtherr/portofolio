Overview

Franca is an AI workspace where engineering teams ask questions about their data in plain English and get answers across SQL and NoSQL databases, without writing queries by hand.

I co-founded it as my SUTD Entrepreneurship Capstone. My focus was the agent workflow, the access controls that keep it safe to point at real databases, and the user research behind the concept.

What I did

  • Co-founded Franca and designed the agent workflow and access controls: per-database grants that expire and a full query audit log.
  • Led user research (a survey of 39 engineers and 40+ sources) and process mapping, then selected the final concept through a weighted decision analysis that stayed robust across 100,000 weightings.
  • Tested the live prototype on AWS (compatible with 36 database engines), turned internal testing into 8 product changes, and defined pilot metrics for AI accuracy and cost.

Result

36database engines supported by the live prototype
