Ngee Ann Kong Si

Scholarship Disbursement Optimisation

A Power BI dashboard, tuition forecasts and an optimisation model for allocating scholarship quotas across 8 faculties.

Role
Data & Business Analytics Consultant
Timeline
Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
Type
Industrial project
Location
Singapore

Overview

Ngee Ann Kong Si awards fixed-value scholarships across 8 university faculties. We built tools to show where disbursements go, forecast tuition, and allocate quotas with an optimisation model.

What I did

  • Produced a Power BI dashboard to visualise disbursement, enrolment and tuition trends across 8 faculties.
  • Forecasted tuition with a Moving Average model (R, Excel) to enable NAK's fixed-value scholarship policy.
  • Proposed an ILP model in Julia (JuMP) to optimise quota allocation based on tuition and past disbursements.

Result

8faculties brought into one dashboard

  • R
  • Excel
  • Julia (JuMP)
  • Integer programming
